What this app keeps on your computer
This app saves your study packs, your progress and your sign-in on this computer so you can keep studying without an internet connection. It is stored in your own user folder and is removed if you uninstall the app and delete its data. Progress you make offline is sent to your Motown AI account the next time you are online.
